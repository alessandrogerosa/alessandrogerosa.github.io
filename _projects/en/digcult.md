Starting from my PhD I have developed multiple research projects related to __digital cultures__ and __digital methods__, mostly in collaboration with other colleagues. While most of these projects have been designed and conducted independently, some overall themes can be identified as follows:

* An analysis of the relationship between users' subjectivities and algorithms in digital platforms, in particular through the conceptualisations of the __algorithmic other__ {% cite gerosa_subjectivity_23 %}, algorithmic public opinion {% cite gandini_algorithmic_2022 %} and of the loop of loops {% cite giorgi_loop_2024 %}.
* An exploration of the impact of digital visual cultures on subcultural aesthetics {% cite giorgi_brnbq_2022 %} and political phenomena {% cite gerosa_memetic_2021 %}.
* A number of methodological contributions, to study how the algorithmic recommendation system of TikTok evolves over time {% cite bruschi_approccio_2024 %} and on Twitter {% cite keeling_tracce_2023 gerosa_new_2022 %}
