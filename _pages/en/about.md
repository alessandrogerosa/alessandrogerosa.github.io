I'm an assistant professor in `cultural sociology` at the department of social and political science at the University of Milan.

My research adopts a cultural and critical perspective to analyse how individuals seek meaningful and authentic lives in late modern capitalism, through `working` and `consumption` practices.

I'm the author of [The Hipster Economy: Taste and Authenticity in Late Modern Capitalism](https://www.uclpress.co.uk/products/230904), published in Open Access by UCL Press (the book's pdf is freely available at the provided link). You can access all the articles and podcasts discussing my book in the dedicated page.

My existing research projects focused on `neo-craft` consumption and work, on `digital cultures` and on `urban transformation` processes. My current (self-funded) research project analyses the `organisational` and `collective` dimension in `politicised` forms of neo-craft work, through the case study of networks and collaborations between `critical winemakers` and other actors.
